Arrhythmia Arcade privacy policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

What data we collect

None. Arrhythmia Arcade does not ask for an account, name or email, and does not send information about you or your games to any server.

What is stored on your device

Your high score and your sound and language preferences are stored only in your browser or device. You can delete them by clearing the site data or uninstalling the app.

Third-party services

The typeface is downloaded from Google Fonts. When it loads, Google receives the usual technical data of any web visit, such as your IP address. We use no advertising or analytics tools.

Educational use

The strips are synthetic and the game is for educational purposes only. It is not a medical device and must not be used to diagnose or treat patients.

Changes

If the game collects data in the future, for example for a research study with informed consent, this page will be updated before it does.
